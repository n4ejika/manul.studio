02 / TRUST

Before booking, patients decide whom to trust with their health.

01 / PERSON

Real practitioners

A name and position are not enough. The patient needs to see specialization, experience, approach and the practitioner’s connection to the relevant service.

02 / PLACE

A clinic you can imagine

Interiors, equipment, navigation and the team reduce uncertainty before the first visit. Stock imagery does not provide this evidence.

03 / CLARITY

No inflated promises

Clear explanations, cost factors and an honest next step create more trust than guaranteed outcomes and advertising superlatives.

03 / PHOTO PRODUCTION

05 / LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

The website is designed for a regulated field.

323-FZ / 118n

Clinic and practitioners

We provide places for required information about the organisation, medical activity, practitioners, education and qualifications.

DECREE 736 / 659 FROM 01.09.2026

Paid services

Licence details, service and price information, terms and other consumer information are built into the structure.

38-FZ / ART. 24

Medical advertising

Advertising blocks are checked for prohibited promises, treatment claims and the required contraindication notice.

152-FZ

Personal data

Forms, consent, privacy policy, integrations and storage geography are included in the technical checklist.

Manul assembles and implements the required information. The clinic remains the source of medical and legal data and approves the final wording, with its legal adviser where required.

Do you start with a page count?+

No. We first study demand, services, geography and business priorities.

Is basic SEO included in launch?+

Yes. Research, search-ready structure and baseline optimization are part of commercial launch.

How is the cost of a medical website calculated?+

The estimate follows the weighted page scope and product complexity. Expert content, required disclosures and additional reviews increase the workload; photography is billed separately.

Yes. Regulated content and required disclosures increase research, editorial and review work.
