METHOD / 03

Define the product boundary.
Then build the website.

01

Separate demand

Do not mix accounting, business registration and a regulated nationwide product in one navigation system.

02

Preserve context

Repeat work should not restart from zero: client knowledge becomes part of the next product.

03

Measure separately

Region, domain and maturity define a separate baseline. Results are not inherited from another website.

04

Develop the system

SEO, advertising and content connect to a working architecture and drive the next improvement cycle.
