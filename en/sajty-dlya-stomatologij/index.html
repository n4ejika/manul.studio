Not a smile gallery. A treatment decision.

The website must connect a patient’s situation with treatment, a practitioner, evidence and a clear next step.

Discuss a dental website↗

01 / CHOICE

One clinic. Several ways to decide.

A path for a person who does not yet know the procedure.

  • Situation and urgency
  • Relevant directions
  • Trust evidence
  • Appointment path

02 / TRUST

Before choosing treatment, people choose whom to trust.

01 / DOCTOR

Expertise in context

A practitioner page is connected to treatment directions, methods, cases and a clear appointment path.

02 / CLINIC

Visible environment

Real interiors, equipment, sterilisation and team workflow help a patient assess the clinic before visiting.

03 / DECISION

Understandable treatment

We explain stages, alternatives, cost factors and limits without promising a guaranteed medical outcome.

03 / PHOTO PRODUCTION

Stock smiles cannot prove this clinic.

Professional photography is mandatory for a dental website. We prepare a shot list for practitioners, treatment rooms, equipment, team and real patient routes. Photography is scoped and billed separately from website development.

01Practitioners: portrait, work and communication
02Treatment rooms and diagnostic equipment
03Reception, navigation and the team
04Photo formats for services, cards and advertising
05Publication rights and patient consent

04 / SEARCH ARCHITECTURE

Search demand becomes a treatment map.

01

Problems

Pain, missing tooth, bite, aesthetics and other situations in the patient’s language.

02

Treatments

Implantation, prosthetics, orthodontics, therapy, surgery and hygiene as separate demand clusters.

03

Geography

Clinic catchment, districts, city and the real service area — not a formal location list.

04

Evidence

Practitioners, methods, equipment, cases, reviews and cost factors connected to each treatment.

07

SEO, analytics, advertising setup and QA.

06 / LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

Dental marketing has medical constraints.

323-FZ / 118n

Organisation and doctors

Required clinic, medical activity, education and qualification information is built into the page system.

DECREE 736 → 659 / 01.09.2026

Licence, services and prices

Consumer information and paid-service terms are not hidden in a footer after design is complete.

38-FZ / ART. 24

Claims and advertising

We separate information from advertising, avoid prohibited guarantees and provide space for required notices.

152-FZ

Appointment forms

Consent, privacy policy, data minimisation, integrations and storage are checked before launch.

The clinic supplies and approves medical and legal information. Manul structures and implements it; final legal review remains with the clinic and its adviser where required.

08 / QUESTIONS

Before launch

No. We create a search-ready system and develop it from measured data.
