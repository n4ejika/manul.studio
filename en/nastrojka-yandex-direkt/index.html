FAQ / 07

Before launch,
it should be clear.

Is media spend included in the setup fee?+

No. The setup fee covers our work. Media is paid by the client directly to the advertising platform.

Can you launch ads to an existing website?+

Yes, if the website is ready for paid traffic. Before launch, we review the offer, landing pages, forms, measurement and conversion path. Any required improvements are scoped separately.

Is monthly management mandatory?+

No. The first month is included. After that, you can continue with Manul or manage the configured account yourself.

Can you launch ads for an existing website?+

Yes, if the site is ready for traffic and can measure leads correctly. We review pages, forms, goals and mobile UX before launch.
