Personal data processing policy

This policy explains how information submitted when contacting Manul is handled.

Version dated 22 August 2026.

1. Controller

Manul studio. Primary contact channel: @hellomanul_bot.

2. Data

Name or preferred form of address, Telegram identifier, contact details voluntarily provided by the user, information about the company, project, budget, timing and source parameters.

3. Purposes

Processing an enquiry, preparing a proposal, concluding and performing an agreement, project communication, measurement and protection from abuse.

4. Processing

Data may be collected, recorded, organised, stored, clarified, used, transferred to processors required for communication and project delivery, blocked and deleted.

5. Storage and protection

Data is retained only for as long as required by the stated purposes, contractual duties and applicable law. Reasonable organisational and technical safeguards are applied.

6. Withdrawal

Consent may be withdrawn through @hellomanul_bot. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing performed before it was received.

7. External services

Telegram and other connected services process information under their own terms. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information in the initial enquiry.
