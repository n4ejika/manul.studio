FAQ / 08

Before we discuss
the project.

How does a $5,000 website differ from an $8,300 launch?

$5,000 covers a website of up to 15 weighted pages. $8,300 adds research, baseline SEO, analytics, initial ad setup and a business profile.

Is media spend included?

No. Campaign, analytics and goal setup are included. Media spend is paid by the client directly to the advertising platform.

Are CRM and integrations mandatory?

No. They are added only when genuinely required and estimated separately. The baseline launch is not inflated with speculative functionality.

Which results are guaranteed?

Manul is accountable for the agreed scope, quality, analytics accuracy and transparent decisions. Rankings, leads and revenue also depend on the market, product, budget and business operations.
