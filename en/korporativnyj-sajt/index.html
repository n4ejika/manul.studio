FAQ / 06

Questions before architecture

Why can the page count not be fixed immediately?→

ANSWER / ROUTEArchitecture follows demand, products, audiences and geography. Before research, only a range can be estimated honestly.

What is a weighted page?→

ANSWER / ROUTEA homepage, complex service page, article and utility page require different effort. Weighted scope accounts for type and complexity, not just URL count.

Can development be ordered on its own?→

ANSWER / ROUTEYes, if research, architecture, content, baseline SEO and analytics are already prepared. Without them, development alone does not solve the business task.
