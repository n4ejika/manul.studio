QUESTIONS BEFORE STARTING / 08

Before we start.

Does Manul work with businesses across Canada?+

Yes. Projects are delivered remotely. Before work begins, we agree on convenient meeting times, who approves the work and when each stage will be reviewed, so teams in different Canadian time zones always know what happens next.

Can we order development without SEO and advertising setup?+

Yes. Development-only is a separate option. If usable research, content, analytics and acquisition plans already exist, they are not added to the estimate again.

Are the prices shown in Canadian dollars?+

Yes. Starting prices on this page are in CAD. The written estimate depends on page types, content, functionality, integrations and the launch scope.

Can the website be built in English and French?+

Yes, but a French version is scoped separately. It requires more than literal translation: navigation, page length, search demand, metadata, forms and legal content must be reviewed for the intended market.

How are Canadian privacy and accessibility requirements handled?+

Before development, we identify what every form collects, where the data goes, which analytics tools are needed and which accessibility features the website requires. The applicable law can depend on the province, sector, audience and data collected, so final legal wording and compliance decisions remain with the client and their Canadian legal adviser.

How is website redesign priced?+

A visual update may add 0–10%. Preserving content, URL value and search visibility normally adds 10–20%; complex CMS, form or integration migration may add 20–35%. A difficult legacy system is assessed separately.

Will Manul support a website built by another company?+

Ongoing support is available for websites built by Manul. An existing third-party website can be diagnosed and redesigned, but it is not taken into routine monthly support unchanged.
