QUESTIONS BEFORE STARTING / 08

Before we start.

Does Manul work with companies across the UK?+

Yes. Projects are delivered remotely across the UK. Calls use agreed overlapping hours, while decisions, files and approvals remain documented between meetings.

Can we order website development without the launch package?+

Yes. Development can be purchased separately. If your research, search plan, analytics and acquisition setup are already usable, we will not include them twice.

Are the prices shown in pounds sterling?+

Yes. The UK page shows starting prices in GBP. A written estimate follows once the page types, content, functionality and launch requirements are clear.

How are privacy, cookies and accessibility handled?+

We map forms, tracking, storage technologies and accessibility requirements before development, then implement the agreed technical scope. Your UK legal adviser remains responsible for final legal wording and compliance decisions.

How does website redesign pricing work?+

A visual update may add 0–10%. Preserving content, URL value and search visibility normally adds 10–20%; complex CMS, form or integration migration may add 20–35%. A difficult legacy system is assessed separately.

Will Manul support a website built by another studio?+

Ongoing support is available for websites built by Manul. An existing third-party website can be diagnosed and redesigned, but it is not taken into routine monthly support unchanged.

How long does a UK website project take?+

Timing depends on the number and complexity of pages, content readiness, functionality, integrations and approval speed. The project schedule is agreed after the scope is clear.
