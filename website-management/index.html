06 / BOUNDARIES

Before management starts

Can management start on any website?+

No. Management is available for websites built or systematically rebuilt by Manul.

Does every activity happen every month?+

No. The scope changes according to project data and current priorities.

Are new pages included in the monthly fee?+

No. They are created when evidence shows the need and are scoped separately.

Is advertising budget included?+

No. Media spend is paid separately from management work.
