QUESTIONS BEFORE STARTING / 08

Before we start.

Does Manul work with Singapore businesses remotely?+

Yes. We agree on the project scope, review dates, meeting times and the people responsible for approvals before production starts. The website is delivered remotely without claiming a local Manul office in Singapore.

Can we order website development without SEO or advertising?+

Yes. Development-only is a separate option. Research, baseline SEO, analytics and initial advertising setup are included only when the complete launch is selected or the estimate lists them separately.

Are the prices shown in Singapore dollars?+

Yes. Starting prices on this page are in SGD. The written estimate depends on page types, content, functionality, integrations, migration and the launch services selected.

Can one website serve Singapore and other Southeast Asian markets?+

Yes, but the regional structure must be planned. We identify which services, languages, currencies, proof and enquiry routes are shared and which need a separate market page. We do not create near-duplicate country pages simply to increase the URL count.

Can the website support several languages?+

Yes. Each language is scoped as a content and interface layer, not as automatic translation. Navigation, text length, metadata, forms, search demand and review all need to be planned for the intended audience.

How are data protection and accessibility handled?+

Before development, we map what every form collects, where the data goes, which tracking tools are required and which accessibility controls are included. Final legal wording and compliance decisions remain with the client and their Singapore adviser.

How is website redesign priced?+

A visual update may add 0–10%. Preserving content, URLs and search value normally adds 10–20%; complex CMS, form or integration migration may add 20–35%. A difficult legacy system is diagnosed and estimated separately.
