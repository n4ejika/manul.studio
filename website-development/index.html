FAQ / 08

Before we discuss
the project.

How does a $10,000 website differ from a $15,000 launch?

$10,000 covers a website of up to 15 weighted pages. $15,000 adds research, baseline SEO, analytics, initial ad setup and a business profile.

Is media spend included?

No. Campaign, analytics and goal setup are included. Media spend is paid by the client directly to the advertising platform.

Are CRM and integrations mandatory?

No. They are added only when genuinely required and estimated separately. The baseline launch is not inflated with speculative functionality.

Who prepares the content and materials?

Manul prepares and edits content for the agreed number of pages. The client provides product information, documentation, photographs and expert input; if materials are missing, we define separately what needs to be produced.

What determines the project timeline?

It depends on the number and complexity of pages, material readiness, functionality, integrations and approval speed. After research and structure, we record the scope, stages and project schedule.

Who owns the website after launch?

The website, domain and working access credentials are transferred to the client. The CMS and technical setup are selected for the project; ongoing Manul support is not a condition of ownership.

How does baseline SEO differ from ongoing SEO?

Baseline SEO prepares the new website for indexing through query mapping, metadata, internal links and technical requirements. Ongoing SEO begins after launch and develops semantics, pages, content and visibility.

Which results are guaranteed?

Manul is accountable for the agreed scope, quality, analytics accuracy and transparent decisions. Rankings, leads and revenue also depend on the market, product, budget and business operations.
