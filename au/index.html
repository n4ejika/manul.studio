QUESTIONS BEFORE STARTING / 08

Before we start.

Does Manul work with businesses anywhere in Australia?+

Yes. Projects are delivered remotely. We agree on meeting times, review dates and the people responsible for approval before production begins, including when a team works across several Australian time zones.

Can we order website development without SEO or advertising setup?+

Yes. Development-only is a separate option. If your business already has usable research, content, analytics and an acquisition plan, those services are not added to the estimate again.

Are the prices shown in Australian dollars?+

Yes. Starting prices on this page are in AUD. A written estimate is based on page types, content, functionality, integrations and the launch services you select.

Can one website target several cities or states?+

Yes. We first determine whether customers search by suburb, city, state or nationwide service. Useful regional pages are created where the offer or demand changes; near-duplicate location pages are not added simply to increase the page count.

How are Australian privacy and accessibility requirements handled?+

Before development, we map the personal information collected by forms, the systems receiving it, the analytics tools required and the accessibility features included in the interface. The Privacy Act, Australian Privacy Principles and other requirements can apply differently by organisation, sector and data type, so final legal advice and approval remain with the client and their Australian adviser.

How is website redesign priced?+

A visual update may add 0–10%. Preserving content, URLs and search value normally adds 10–20%; complex CMS, form or integration migration may add 20–35%. A difficult legacy system is diagnosed and estimated separately.

Will Manul support a website built by another company?+

Ongoing support is available for websites built by Manul. An existing third-party website can be diagnosed and redesigned, but it is not taken into routine monthly support unchanged.
