06 / LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

Dental marketing has medical constraints.

323-FZ / 118n

Organisation and doctors

Required clinic, medical activity, education and qualification information is built into the page system.

DECREE 736 / 659 FROM 01.09.2026

Licence, services and prices

Licence details, service and price information and paid-service terms are built into the page structure.

38-FZ / ART. 24

Claims and advertising

We separate information from advertising, avoid prohibited guarantees and provide space for required notices.

152-FZ

Appointment forms

Consent, privacy policy, data minimisation, integrations and storage are checked before launch.

The clinic supplies and approves medical and legal information. Manul structures and implements it; final legal review remains with the clinic and its adviser where required.

08 / QUESTIONS

Before launch

Can a ready-made visual template be used?+

A visual foundation may be reused, but the service structure, local demand, practitioners and trust evidence are designed for the specific clinic.

Do you guarantee search positions?+

No. The launch includes a search-informed structure, technical optimisation and analytics; positions depend on competition, the website and continued development.

How is a dental website priced?+

The estimate follows the weighted page scope and clinic complexity. Expert content and legal review increase the workload; photography is billed separately.

Does advertising come after the site?+

Initial advertising setup is part of commercial launch; media budget is paid separately.
