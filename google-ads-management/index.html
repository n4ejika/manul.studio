FAQ / 07

Key questions before advertising launch.

Is media spend included in the setup fee?+

No. The setup fee covers our work. Media is paid by the client directly to the advertising platform.

Can you launch ads to an existing website?+

Yes, if the website is ready to receive and measure paid traffic correctly. We review the offer, landing pages, forms, measurement and mobile UX. A substantial website rebuild is handled as a separate project.

Is monthly management mandatory?+

No. The first month is included. After that, you can continue with Manul or manage the configured account yourself.

Can you guarantee lead volume or cost?+

No. Demand, competition and an initial budget can be estimated before launch, but actual lead cost is established after data accumulates. We configure measurement, control spend and make decisions from real results.
