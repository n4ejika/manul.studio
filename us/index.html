QUESTIONS BEFORE STARTING / 08

What to clarify before the project starts.

How does remote work across US time zones?+

We agree on overlapping hours for calls and approvals. The remaining work continues asynchronously, with decisions and files documented.

How do reviews and approvals work?+

You approve the structure, one of three design directions and the working pages at clear review points before the project moves forward.

Is the project price fixed?+

The website shows starting prices. After the scope is agreed, you receive an estimate for that work. Additional work is estimated before it is added.

Can we order development only?+

Yes. If usable research, SEO, analytics or advertising setup already exists, it does not need to be purchased twice.

Can Manul support a website built by another company?+

Manul provides ongoing support for websites built by Manul. An existing third-party website can be diagnosed and redesigned, but it is not accepted for routine monthly support.

How are accessibility, privacy and legal requirements handled?+

Relevant requirements are identified before development and the agreed technical measures are included in the project. Final legal wording and compliance decisions must be approved by the client’s US legal advisor.
