QUESTIONS BEFORE STARTING / 08

Before we start.

Does Manul work with companies across all UAE emirates?+

Yes. Projects are delivered remotely. Before work begins, we agree on meeting times, review dates and the people responsible for approval.

Can we order development without SEO or advertising setup?+

Yes. Development-only is a separate option. If usable research, content, analytics and an acquisition plan already exist, they are not added to the estimate again.

Are the prices shown in UAE dirhams?+

Yes. Starting prices are shown in AED. A written estimate depends on page types, content, functionality, integrations and the launch services selected.

Is Arabic just a translation of the English website?+

No. Arabic localization is scoped separately because it needs editing, RTL behaviour, form and metadata review, content-length checks and interface QA.

How is website redesign priced?+

A visual update may add 0–10%. Preserving content, URL value and search visibility normally adds 10–20%; complex CMS, form or integration migration may add 20–35%. A difficult legacy system is assessed separately.

Does Manul guarantee legal compliance?+

We map requirements and implement the agreed technical controls. Final legal wording and compliance decisions remain with the client and their UAE legal adviser.
