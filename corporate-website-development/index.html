FAQ / 06

Key questions about corporate websites

Who needs a corporate website?→

ANSWERCompanies with several services, products, audiences, branches or regions. If there is one offer for one audience, a landing page is often enough.

What determines structure and cost?→

ANSWERDemand, the number of business lines and audiences, geography, page types and product complexity all matter. A range can be estimated before research; the final page list follows it.

What is included and what is estimated separately?→

ANSWERThe launch includes research, architecture, content, prototypes, a design system, responsive development, baseline SEO, analytics and initial advertising setup. Media spend, complex functionality, user accounts and integrations are estimated separately.
